JOR 4.0 — Canonical synthetic demo script (~5 minutes)
======================================================

LOCKED CONFIG (do not change for the pitch)
  scenario = baseline
  seed     = 42
  units    = 6
  skill    = expert
  policy   = intervene
  ticks    = 120

START
  python3 run_demo.py
  → open the printed URL
  → click "Live stream"
  → press M (Map focus)

MINUTE 0–1  Opening picture
  Green readiness bars, low NHP (~0.05), OSM dark map with TF symbols.
  Point out: TF 1-6 INF, TF 3-8 AR, TRP A RCN, SPT CO LOG.
  Say: "Force is healthy; decision support is quiet."

MINUTE 1–2  First decision
  Right rail shows PRESSURE_EARLY / similar with T-minus.
  Click [1] Reinforce / reduce tempo (intervene policy).
  Say: "Commander intervenes under time pressure."

MINUTE 2–3  Local strain (t≈30)
  TF card highlights degraded (e.g. TF 3-8 AR *).
  Readiness dips on that unit; risk ring tightens.
  Say: "Local friction appears without force-wide collapse."

MINUTE 3–4  Pressure climb
  NHP and θc rise; ALERT may arm; heat / red rings expand.
  Optional: press ] to show decision rail if Map focus was on.

MINUTE 4–5  Catastrophic stress (t≈110)
  Deep red rings, stacked cues, low readiness fills.
  Say: "Overextension + coordination failure — NHP peaks."

CLOSE
  After-action card appears (UI) and prints in the terminal:
    final state, peak NHP, decisions, degraded units.
  Say: "Same seed → same arc. Demo is repeatable."

KEYS
  [  toggle readiness matrix
  ]  toggle decision panel
  M  map only / restore panels

OFFLINE (no stream)
  python3 run_demo.py --offline
  python3 run_demo.py --playback
